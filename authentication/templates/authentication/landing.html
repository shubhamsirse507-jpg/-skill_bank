❓ Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started on SkillBank.

Is SkillBank really 100% free?
Yes! SkillBank is built on the philosophy of reciprocity. You give your time and knowledge in one discipline, and receive expert tutoring in another. No cash transaction is required.
What if I'm a complete beginner?
Everyone is good at something! You don't need to be a world-class professional. You can teach beginner-level guitar, basic cooking, conversational native language, or workout motivation.
How do live exchange sessions work?
Once you and your partner accept a swap request, you can use our built-in messaging to pick a time and launch an interactive live video room directly inside SkillBank.
How does Skye AI help me?
Skye can help you craft compelling skill descriptions, break down curriculum topics into bite-sized lessons, and suggest matching skill swaps tailored to your goals.
